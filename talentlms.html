TalentLMS Implementation Project

Selecting and implementing TalentLMS to enable scalable, asynchronous online training for early childhood educators at our nonprofit.

Role: Instructional Designer & LMS Lead Tools: TalentLMS, eLearning authoring tools, SCORM

Project Overview

As part of expanding professional learning for early childhood educators, I led the research, evaluation, and implementation of a learning management system that could support asynchronous, self-paced online training. Through stakeholder interviews, use case mapping, and vendor evaluation criteria, TalentLMS emerged as the best fit for our organization’s instructional needs, budget constraints, and technical capacity. The platform supported scalable course delivery, intuitive navigation for learners, and robust reporting for program staff.


Selecting and implementing the LMS represented only one phase of this initiative. A critical next stage involved establishing a sustainable instructional design framework to ensure consistency, quality, and scalability across all online trainings. Using an iterative interpretation of the ADDIE model, I developed a flexible design framework that emphasized continuous analysis, rapid prototyping, and data-informed revisions. I then trained the instructional design team on applying this framework within TalentLMS, aligning course development processes, templates, and review cycles. This approach not only standardized design practices across the organization but also empowered designers to iterate efficiently while maintaining a learner-centered, evidence-based focus.

The Challenge

The organization lacked a formal LMS, resulting in fragmented training delivery, inconsistent learner experience, and limited tracking of completion and outcomes. We needed a solution that met diverse user needs (from novice to advanced educators), supported significant asynchronous content, and could be maintained with limited technical staffing.


Our primary learners were early childhood educators balancing full-time work responsibilities with family and caregiving commitments outside of their professional roles. As a result, participation in in-person and even synchronous virtual trainings proved challenging, leading to enrollment and attendance barriers. Through learner needs assessments and participation data, we identified a strong demand for flexible, self-paced online training that could be completed on individual schedules. Addressing this need required a learning solution that prioritized accessibility, flexibility, and ease of use while maintaining instructional quality.

Design & Approach

  • Conducted needs analysis, including surveys and interviews with staff and learners to define key LMS requirements.
  • Developed an LMS evaluation matrix with criteria such as user experience, accessibility, reporting, cost, and support.
  • Benchmarked multiple LMS platforms against the matrix, compiling pros/cons and demo insights.
  • Facilitated vendor demos with cross-functional stakeholders to validate system usability and fit.
  • Selected TalentLMS based on alignment with requirements, ease of use, and scalability.
  • Established a standardized instructional design framework grounded in iterative ADDIE principles:
    • Analyze: Defined learner profiles, constraints, and performance goals for each training.
    • Design: Created reusable course blueprints, learning objectives, and assessment strategies.
    • Develop: Built modular, accessible content within TalentLMS using standardized templates.
    • Implement: Launched courses through phased rollouts, including pilot testing.
    • Evaluate: Collected learner feedback, completion data, and assessment results to inform revisions.
  • Built initial course templates, configured user roles, and designed onboarding documentation aligned to the framework.
  • Conducted facilitator and pilot user training to ensure consistent application of the framework prior to organization-wide launch.

Outcomes & Impact

  • Successfully launched TalentLMS to 500+ early childhood professionals with positive feedback on usability.
  • Achieved a 45% increase in on-time course completion within 6 months.
  • Enhanced reporting and learner analytics enabled data-informed improvements to curriculum design.
  • Reduced administrative overhead for training coordination by automating enrollment and tracking.
  • Created reusable LMS templates and documentation for ongoing course development.
