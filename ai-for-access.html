AI for Access

A statewide-impact initiative leveraging ethical, AI-supported course design to strengthen accessibility, instructional quality, and faculty capacity in online and hybrid learning.

Role: Co-Project Director & Lead Instructional Designer Tools: Canvas, Generative AI, Accessibility Auditing Tools, Quality Matters

Project Overview

AI for Access: Transforming Teaching and Learning through Innovative Course Design is a two-year, $500,000 competitively awarded grant-funded initiative, authored and led by MacKenzie Corrigan, that positions Rose State College as a leader in responsible, scalable use of Artificial Intelligence to improve accessibility and online course quality.


The project responds to growing demands for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, faculty AI literacy, and consistent instructional design across modalities. By embedding AI-supported workflows within the Academic Technology and Teaching Innovation Center (ATTIC), AI for Access establishes a sustainable framework that improves student access, reduces manual remediation, and strengthens faculty confidence in AI-enhanced teaching.

The Challenge

At Rose State College, all courses rely on the Learning Management System, regardless of modality. As online enrollment expanded, existing manual processes for accessibility remediation, document review, and course quality checks became increasingly unsustainable.

Faculty faced rising expectations to design accessible, high-quality courses while navigating unfamiliar AI tools without coordinated guidance or institutional guardrails. Without a structured approach, AI adoption risked inconsistency, inefficiency, and diminished instructional integrity.

Design & Approach

  • Established a dedicated AI and Accessibility Coordinator to research, pilot, and scale AI-supported accessibility and design tools.
  • Implemented a research–pilot–scale model to vet AI tools for FERPA compliance, accessibility impact, and instructional value.
  • Integrated Universal Design for Learning (UDL) and Quality Matters standards into AI-supported course design workflows.
  • Designed a modular professional development program, including AI exploration sessions, hands-on faculty workshops, and division-based AI Champion cohorts.
  • Embedded ethical AI principles—human oversight, transparency, bias mitigation, and data privacy—into all training and implementation activities.

Outcomes & Impact

  • Increased faculty confidence and proficiency in AI-supported accessibility and course design practices.
  • Improved consistency and efficiency in meeting WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards across online and hybrid courses.
  • Reduced time spent on manual remediation through AI-assisted workflows and accessibility auditing.
  • Created the AI for Access Toolkit, including vetted tools, templates, and implementation guides for institutional and statewide use.
  • Positioned Rose State College as a convener and model for ethical AI integration through a statewide AI for Access Showcase and cross-institutional collaboration.
