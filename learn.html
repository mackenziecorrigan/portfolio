LEARN Repository

Designing high-quality, research-based instructional resources for educators through collaborative, large-scale curriculum development.

Organization: K20 Center, University of Oklahoma Role: Instructional Designer Focus: Lesson design, instructional strategies, and educator resources

Project Overview

The LEARN Repository is a public-facing digital collection of educator resources developed by the K20 Center at the University of Oklahoma. The repository includes hundreds of classroom-ready lessons, instructional strategies, technology tools, and professional learning resources designed to support engaging, research-based instruction across grade levels and content areas.


As an instructional designer, I collaborated closely with subject matter experts (SMEs), curriculum writers, artists, and media specialists to design, develop, and publish resources that aligned with instructional best practices, state standards, and K20’s signature instructional framework. My work contributed to a large catalog of published projects used by educators nationwide.

The Challenge

The scale and diversity of the LEARN Repository presented unique design challenges. Resources needed to be pedagogically sound, visually engaging, classroom-tested, and adaptable to a wide range of instructional contexts. Each project required coordination across multiple roles while maintaining consistency in instructional quality, structure, and usability.


Additionally, lessons and tools needed to be easily discoverable and intuitive for educators, many of whom accessed resources independently without formal training or facilitation. Ensuring clarity, alignment, and practical classroom application was critical to the success of each published resource.

Design & Approach

  • Collaborated with subject matter experts to translate content knowledge into engaging, learner-centered instructional materials.
  • Applied research-based instructional strategies aligned with the K20 instructional framework, including inquiry-based learning and student engagement strategies.
  • Worked cross-functionally with writers, artists, and media specialists to ensure visual clarity, accessibility, and instructional coherence.
  • Designed lesson structures, instructional strategies, and educator-facing guidance to support ease of classroom implementation.
  • Reviewed and refined resources through iterative feedback cycles to ensure alignment with quality standards prior to publication.
  • Contributed to the development and publication of a large body of LEARN Repository projects, documented in a comprehensive project tracking spreadsheet.

Outcomes & Impact

  • Published numerous instructional resources used by educators nationwide
  • Supported consistent, high-quality instructional design across a large-scale repository
  • Strengthened collaboration between instructional designers, SMEs, and creative teams
  • Enhanced educator access to research-based, classroom-ready materials
  • Contributed to a sustainable model for ongoing curriculum development and publication
