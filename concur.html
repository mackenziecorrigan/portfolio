HR Training

Designing a self-paced, role-agnostic reference course to help all employees navigate the end-to-end travel process in Concur.

Role: Instructional Designer Tools: Concur, eLearning authoring tools, screen recordings, job aids, SCORM

Project Overview

To support employees across the organization in planning, booking, and reconciling travel, I designed a self-paced Concur Travel Training course that functions as both onboarding and an ongoing reference resource. The course organizes complex travel policies, system workflows, and common troubleshooting scenarios into a clear, modular learning experience that users can return to as needed.


Beyond creating a single course, this project established a reusable structure for future HR systems training. Using an iterative ADDIE-informed approach, I aligned learning objectives to real workplace tasks, prototyped navigation and visuals, and refined content based on stakeholder feedback and usability testing. The result is a scalable model that can be updated as travel policies or Concur features change.

The Challenge

Employees had inconsistent knowledge of Concur and the organization’s travel policies, leading to booking errors, delayed reimbursements, and increased support requests to HR and Finance. Existing guidance was scattered across documents and emails rather than centralized in one accessible training resource.


Learners represented a wide range of experience levels—from first-time travelers to frequent travelers—and worked in different roles with varying approval workflows. The solution needed to be flexible, searchable, and usable on demand, while also clearly communicating compliance requirements and best practices.

Design & Approach

  • Reviewed travel policies, Concur configurations, and common help desk tickets to identify high-priority learning needs.
  • Mapped the end-to-end travel process (request → booking → travel → expense report → approval → reimbursement).
  • Defined clear, task-based learning objectives aligned to real employee workflows.
  • Created a modular course structure so users could complete sections in any order or revisit specific topics.
  • Developed step-by-step system demonstrations using annotated screen recordings and guided practice activities.
  • Designed concise job aids (checklists, quick reference guides, and decision trees) to support performance after training.
  • Applied an iterative design process:
    • Analyze: Identified learner roles, pain points, and critical tasks in Concur.
    • Design: Outlined course flow, visuals, and assessment strategies.
    • Develop: Built accessible, mobile-friendly content in an authoring tool.
    • Implement: Piloted with a small group of employees and refined based on feedback.
    • Evaluate: Gathered usability feedback and tracked help desk trends post-launch.
  • Partnered with HR and Finance stakeholders to ensure accuracy and alignment with current travel policies.

Outcomes & Impact

  • Launched a centralized, self-paced Concur Travel Training course accessible to all employees.
  • Reduced recurring "how-to" questions to HR/Finance by providing clear, searchable guidance.
  • Improved accuracy of travel bookings and expense reports through step-by-step demonstrations.
  • Created reusable templates and job aids that can be updated as policies or systems change.
  • Established a scalable model for future HR systems training (e.g., onboarding, benefits, or payroll tools).
