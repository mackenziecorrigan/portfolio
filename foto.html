From Obligation to Opportunity

A comprehensive accessibility learning series designed to empower faculty to create inclusive, compliant, and learner-centered online courses.

Program: Accessibility Champion Program Role: Lead Instructional Designer Delivery: In-person workshops & self-paced online learning

Project Overview

From Obligation to Opportunity is a multi-session accessibility learning series developed as the core professional learning component of the Accessibility Champion Program. The series reframes accessibility from a compliance-driven obligation into an instructional design opportunity that enhances learning for all students.


Designed for faculty teaching in Canvas, the series blends facilitated workshops with an online self-paced course to accommodate varied schedules, experience levels, and instructional contexts. Together, these learning experiences build faculty confidence and capability in designing accessible, inclusive online courses aligned with ADA, Section 508, WCAG 2.2, and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles.

The Challenge

Due to the DOJ ruling regarding web content accessibility, faculty were increasingly expected to meet accessibility standards, yet many experienced accessibility as overwhelming, highly technical, or purely compliance-focused. Prior training efforts often emphasized remediation over intentional instructional design, resulting in surface-level fixes rather than sustainable practices.


Additionally, accessibility training needed to support faculty with a wide range of experience—from novice online instructors to advanced Canvas users— while fitting into demanding teaching schedules. A single workshop approach was insufficient; faculty needed a structured learning pathway that supported skill-building over time, application within their own courses, and accountability through implementation and review.

Design & Approach

The learning series was intentionally designed as a scaffolded, blended experience that combined conceptual foundations, hands-on practice, and reflective implementation.

  • Designed and facilitated five interactive training sessions:
    • Foundations of Accessibility
    • Accessibility in Canvas
    • Accessible Documents and Media
    • Designing for All Learners
    • Evaluating Accessibility with Tools
  • Developed the self-paced Canvas course Design with POURpose, introducing accessibility through the POUR principles (Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust) and core accessibility skills.
  • Applied an iterative, ADDIE-informed design framework emphasizing analysis, rapid prototyping, and continuous improvement.
  • Created reusable templates, checklists, and job aids to support immediate application within faculty members’ own courses.
  • Embedded implementation requirements and course reviews to reinforce accountability and instructional quality.

Outcomes & Impact

  • Increased faculty confidence in designing accessible Canvas content
  • Documented accessibility improvements through course audits and reviews
  • Credentialed a cohort of Accessibility Champions across academic divisions
  • Established a scalable, repeatable model for ongoing accessibility professional development
  • Shifted faculty mindset from reactive remediation to proactive, inclusive design
