About Me

Instructional designer with a passion for creating learner-centered, accessible, and engaging learning experiences. I combine pedagogy, technology, and AI to transform teaching and learning.

My Story

I began my career as an elementary school teacher in St. Petersburg, Florida. During my time as an educator, the turmoil of the COVID-19 pandemic was in full swing. As a new teacher with a vision for my long-term career in education, I saw this as an opportunity to join the movement of online learning by getting a master's degree in Educational Technology. During my time in graduate school, I found my passion for instructional design. This altered the trajectory of my career and ultimately led me to accepting my first position as instructional designer for early childhood educators. Since then, I have worked in various contexts as an instructional designer. Currently, I am the Director of Academic Technology and Teaching Innovation at Rose State College where I support faculty in enhancing the learning experience for students and supporting effective use and implementation of academic technologies in their courses.

While I am incredibly passionate about educational technology and learning design, I also have many hobbies and interests outside of this work. At home, I have a husband, who I met while in undergrad, and a sweet Goldendoodle named Kirby! We live in our beautiful forever home in Oklahoma City. I enjoy a variety of creative and adventurous pursuits, including crocheting, coloring, and sewing, as well as reading, exploring new places, cooking, baking, and (of course) a bit of shopping.
